---

I build platforms, communities, and music. By day I am **Platform Governance Lead at Nationale-Nederlanden España**, where I created the Platform Engineering team, led API governance and company-wide CI/CD standardization, and organize NN Tech Talks, 70+ so far.

In November 2024 I founded **Arcasiles Group**, a cultural-tech platform in Vega Baja that spans tech, events, music, and community. I speak on AI, DevSecOps, and Platform Engineering, and write the newsletter "Entre código y personas".

Away from the keyboard I play guitar in Vega Soul Band and JALEO.

📫 Reach me on [X](https://x.com/soydachi) or by [email](mailto:info@arcasiles.com).
